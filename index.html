📖 Reading · Our Saturday Places

Original A1–A2 story · Present Simple only

Noah is fourteen. He lives near a skatepark and a small café. He usually goes to the skatepark on Saturdays. There are two tables outside the café, and there is a bookshop across the street.

His friend Emma likes sports, but she doesn’t like skating. She can ride a bike, but she can’t skate. She usually reads at the bookshop. Emma has a red backpack and a blue jacket. Noah has a water bottle and a sandwich in his bag.

“Do you like this café?” asks Emma. “Yes, I do,” says Noah. “It has great sandwiches!” Emma laughs. “Those tables are nice, too. Let’s sit there.” Noah agrees. He often buys juice, but he doesn’t buy soda. They talk about school, music and their favourite places.
